## New design decisions (discussed, not yet built)

He asked to **discuss and think first — no code until he says "ابدأ اشتغل".**

- **Background:** dark / space. Not light.
- **Languages:** Arabic + English + Russian, switchable from a top picker. The
  name itself retypes in the chosen language.
- **Terminal/data aesthetic:** *name on the home page only* — nothing more.
  Plus data/numbers. He explicitly does NOT want it everywhere.
- **Name treatment:** types itself like a terminal readout (like a programmer /
  data scientist). Must be fast (~600–900ms), then stop and leave a blinking
  cursor. Cursor blink = the "always alive" signature, cheaper than retyping.
  Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; name must be in the DOM from the start.
- **One-liner under the name:** must be *true*, not aspirational cartoon. He
  rejected "coming soon to analyze stars" as stupid. He is **still learning**.
- **Home:** night sky. **Volleyball:** completely different world —
  **orange / white / black, white dominant**, and explicitly *not* rustic or
  tacky (he said "مش عبيط ولا فلاحي"). Suggested orange duotone on the 17
  tournament photos to unify mismatched phone shots into one system.
- **Cohesion solution:** pages share the same *grammar* (grid, type, motion,
  texture) but not the same *palette*. "Same system, different light." Home is
  night, volleyball is day.
- **Gradients:** he wants every page to have a soft gradient of its OWN core
  colours, so the browser feels the site is alive and each page feels like a
  different place. Page transitions must feel like continuous movement, NOT
  like jumping to a different website.
- **Contact page:** email + CV download + social links. No contact form.
- **Who sees it:** the real target is a **Russian state scholarship** — that is
  his first destination. So Russian is the highest-priority language to get
  right, not the third one. Technical university committees respond well to
  a precise "research console" look; the anime flair belongs in the ambient
  sky, NOT in the UI chrome.

**He is extremely strict about:** wasted space, big empty gaps, orphan lines,
garbled Arabic/Russian text, and spacing rhythm. He said to review these errors
"زي اسمك" (like your own name). Promise him a trilingual side-by-side review
page so he can proofread everything in one place.
